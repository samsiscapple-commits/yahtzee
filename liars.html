Liar's Dice

Both players start with five hidden dice. Each round, everyone's dice reroll and the loser of the last round bids first.

A bid claims how many dice of one face are on the table in total, both hands combined — "three 4s" means at least three 4s exist across all dice. Ones are wild and count as every face (except when the bid itself is on 1s).

On your turn: raise the bid (more dice, or the same count on a higher face) or call Liar! All dice reveal. If the bid holds, the caller loses a die; if it falls short, the bidder does.

Lose all five and you've lost the game. Bluff early, count wilds, and watch how fast they bid.
