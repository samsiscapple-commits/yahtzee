The Odds

200,000 simulated turns, played the greedy way: chase one face, reroll the rest, three rolls. No adviser at the table — just what the dice actually do over a long night.

Upper section: what a turn is really worth

Green bar is the average you score chasing that face. Red line is par (three of a kind). Every face pays slightly under par — which is why the bonus is hard, and why sixes carry it.
